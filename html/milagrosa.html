Hospital La Milagrosa

Providing quality healthcare services to the region.

Back to Health Network

Location: Carrera 5, Silvia, Cauca.

Accessibility: Facilities adapted for people with reduced mobility.

Medical Services:

  • 24-hour emergency care
  • Hospitalization for adults and children
  • General surgery
  • Intensive care units for neonatal, pediatric, and adult patients

Infrastructure: Equipped with incubators, pediatric modules, and areas designed to provide comfort and safety to patients.

This hospital plays a key role in providing primary and specialized care to the Misak Indigenous and mestizo population of the region. It serves as a pillar of public health in a rural area with limited access to high-complexity medical centers.
