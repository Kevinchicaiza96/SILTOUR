Restaurante y Estadero La Playa

Located near the river, this classic spot is known for its grilled fish, seafood dishes, and family-friendly atmosphere. Perfect for sunny weekends with live music and cold drinks.

Address: Via Silvia – Laguna de Silvia Road, Cauca

Hours: Tue–Sun: 11:00 AM – 10:00 PM (Closed Mondays)

Get Directions

Trucha y Sazón

Traditional restaurant known for its fresh trout, cooked with local spices and served with patacones and arepas de maíz. Cozy and offers a beautiful view of the countryside.

Address: Calle 6 #3-15, Silvia, Cauca

Hours: Tue–Sun: 12:00 PM – 8:00 PM

Get Directions

Parrilla Paisa

Restaurant and café near the lagoon where you can enjoy local coffee, pastries, and handmade desserts. Ideal for relaxing after visiting the market or the lake.

Address: Near Laguna de Silvia, Cauca

Hours: Every day: 8:00 AM – 7:00 PM

Get Directions

Restaurante Antojos, sabor y tradición

One of the town’s favorite spots for regional food. Serves tamales, empanadas de pipián, and caldo de gallina criolla. The authentic taste of Silvia’s culinary tradition.

Address: Calle 5 #2-22, Silvia, Cauca

Hours: Mon–Sat: 11:00 AM – 8:30 PM

Get Directions

Birimbí de la casa de Los Sueños

Located on the hills surrounding Silvia, this restaurant offers traditional Colombian food, grilled dishes, and incredible mountain views. A great place for photos and outdoor dining.

Address: 1 km along the road to El Resguardo, Silvia, Cauca

Hours: Fri–Sun: 10:00 AM – 9:00 PM

Get Directions

Restaurante Y Pesca El Paraiso

Perfect for a family outing. You can fish for fresh trout and have it prepared instantly, or choose from a menu of traditional mountain dishes. Enjoy the open air and views.

Address: Vereda San Pedro, near the Reservoir, Silvia, Cauca

Hours: Sat–Sun: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM

Get Directions
