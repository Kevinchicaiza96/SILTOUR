Misak University (Ala Kusreik Ya)

Represents the self-determination of the Misak people through education. With support from the Ministry of Education, it develops programs strengthening indigenous identity and intercultural dialogue.

Location: Resguardo Misak, Silvia, Cauca

Type: Community Indigenous University
