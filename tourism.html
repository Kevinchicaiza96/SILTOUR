Discover Silvia's Top Attractions

Experience the culture, nature, and unique charm of Silvia, Cauca.

Mercado Indígena

The Misak (Guambiana) Community

The main participants in this market are the Misak indigenous people...

Traditional Attire

  • Men and women wear anaco (dark blue/black skirts)
  • Ruanas or ponchos in intense blue
  • Traditional hats (low-crowned or black felt)
  • Every garment represents connection to Pachamama

Market Experience

  • Agricultural Products: fruits, vegetables, panela, potatoes, onions, cassava
  • Handicrafts: wool textiles like mochilas, ponchos, chumbes
  • Gastronomy: sweets, typical dishes, strawberries with cream

Parque Principal

Heart of the town and cultural hub...

  • Natural setting with green spaces
  • Colonial & Andean architecture
  • Market days and local social life

Colina de Belén

Panoramic viewpoint of Silvia

  • Short walk from Main Square
  • Chapel at the top
  • 360-degree views of town, mountains, and rural landscapes

Lago Chimán

Peaceful Andean lake

  • Walking trails, meditation spots, photography
  • Nearby rustic restaurants
  • Starting point for ecotourism activities
